Frequently Asked

The quick answers

How do I add a new product?

Go to Products and click “Add Product”. Fill in the name, category, price and stock, then save — it appears in your catalogue instantly.

How do I invite a team member?

Open Roles & Access and click “Invite Member”. Enter their email and choose a role (Admin, Editor or Viewer). They receive an invite link to join.

Can I export my reports?

Yes. On the Reports page every card has a Download button for PDF or CSV. Recently generated exports are listed below for re-download.

How do I switch to dark or light mode?

Use the sun/moon toggle in the top bar, or the Dark mode switch under Settings → Appearance. Your choice is remembered on this device.

How is my billing handled?

Head to Billing to view your current plan, update your payment method and download past invoices at any time.
